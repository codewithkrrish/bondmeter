    How it works

    BondMeter turns two names and a mode into a score with a stable hash — the same pair in the same mode always gets the same percentage, whichever order you type the names in. The breakdown bars (chemistry, banter, trust, adventure) come from the same hash, so they are stable too.

    This is just for fun. It is not real compatibility science, it cannot predict a relationship, and no score here means anything about two real people. Use it for laughs, ice-breakers, and friendly arguments — not for decisions that matter.

    Everything runs in your browser. Your names and your recent pairs are stored in this browser's local storage and never leave your device. Clearing your browser data clears them too.
